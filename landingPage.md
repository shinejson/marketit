# Frequently Asked Questions

## Everything You Need to Know

### What is MarketHub?

MarketHub is an all-in-one SaaS platform that allows businesses to create, customize and manage their own online stores.

### Do I need coding knowledge?

No. MarketHub is designed to make store creation and customization accessible without requiring advanced coding knowledge.

### Can I customize my store?

Yes. You can customize your store's branding, pages, layout, colors, typography, images and other design elements using the MarketHub Page Builder.

### What is the Page Builder?

The Page Builder is a visual drag-and-drop tool that allows you to create and customize pages without manually writing code.

### Are templates available?

Yes. MarketHub will provide both free and premium templates through the Template Marketplace.

### Can I purchase premium templates?

Yes. Selected templates can be purchased separately and customized for your store.

### Can I change my template?

Your ability to change templates can depend on the template and your MarketHub plan. Your store content should remain separate from the template design so your products and business data are protected.

### Can I sell different types of products?

Yes. MarketHub is designed to support businesses selling different types of products across multiple categories.

### Can customers shop from mobile devices?

Yes. MarketHub storefronts are designed to support responsive layouts across desktop, tablet and mobile devices.

### Can I manage my orders?

Yes. Your MarketHub dashboard provides tools for viewing and managing customer orders.

### Can I manage my products?

Yes. You can create products, organize categories, manage pricing and maintain your product catalog.

### Can I have more than one store?

Depending on your MarketHub plan, you may be able to create and manage multiple stores.

### How do payments work?

MarketHub can support integrated payment methods so customers can pay for products through your online store. Available payment options will depend on the payment providers supported by your MarketHub setup.
